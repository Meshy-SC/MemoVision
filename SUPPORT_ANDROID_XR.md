# MemoVision Support — Android XR

MemoVision is a card-matching puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

MemoVision opens as a window in your space. Look at a card and pinch to turn it over (or point with your hand or a controller and pinch).

- **The goal:** find every matching pair. Each level opens with every card face up for a moment, then turns them face down.
- **Turn cards over:** turn over two cards: a matching pair stays face up, the rest turn back.
- **Stars:** three stars for clearing a board within par, the flips a careful player needs (about one mismatch for every two pairs); two within 20 % of par; one otherwise.
- **Lives:** each level allows a number of wrong matches, shown as Lives at the top. Run out and the level is lost; **Try Again** deals it again.
- **Twists:** later levels add a clock and a flip budget (top right), and some worlds have a rule of their own: Timed Peek, Mirage (decoy cards with no twin), Chain Combo, Freeze (cards flip back on their own), Shuffle (cards trade places), Mixed and Lava (some positions flip back faster). The line under the top bar names the level's twists.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb turns every card face up again for a few seconds. It uses one reveal credit the first time you reveal a level; after that it's free for that level.
- **Restart** deals the level again. **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, each level opens when you clear the one before it.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, MemoVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Reveal credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in MemoVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install MemoVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears completed levels and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
